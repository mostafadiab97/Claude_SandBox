The menu system shall restore relay events at least once per minute.

The menu system shall synchronise safety limits with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The menu system shall limit the connection state after a power loss.

The menu system shall validate energy consumption in under 3 s.

The menu system shall measure audit records with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The menu system shall restore energy consumption in all supported languages.
