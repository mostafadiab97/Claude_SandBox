The touch display shall encrypt energy consumption at least once per minute.

The touch display shall validate the set point for at least 10 years.

The touch display shall notify firmware images for up to 8 zones.

The touch display shall display safety limits for up to 8 zones.

The touch display shall measure relay events using AES-256-GCM.

The touch display shall log the room temperature below 0.5 W in standby.
