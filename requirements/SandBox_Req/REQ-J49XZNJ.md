The notification service shall synchronise relay events when the network is unavailable.

The notification service shall display safety limits when the network is unavailable.

The notification service shall switch energy consumption with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The notification service shall encrypt access tokens in all supported languages.

The notification service shall restore audit records in all supported languages.

The notification service shall detect relay events for at least 10 years.
