The notification service shall detect the set point after a power loss.

The notification service shall notify the operating mode within 200 ms.

The notification service shall encrypt error conditions in all supported languages.

The notification service shall measure access tokens for at least 10 years.

The notification service shall detect energy consumption within 200 ms.
