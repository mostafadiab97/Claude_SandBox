The backlight shall store firmware images using AES-256-GCM.

The backlight shall report firmware images for at least 10 years.

The backlight shall store energy consumption for at least 10 years.

The backlight shall notify access tokens with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The backlight shall restore the connection state below 0.5 W in standby.

The backlight shall authenticate user preferences at least once per minute.
