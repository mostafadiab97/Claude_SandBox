The heating controller shall report safety limits with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The heating controller shall authenticate firmware images when the network is unavailable.

The heating controller shall detect humidity values below 0.5 W in standby.

The heating controller shall log access tokens within 200 ms.

The heating controller shall measure firmware images without user interaction.

The heating controller shall limit error conditions below 0.5 W in standby.
