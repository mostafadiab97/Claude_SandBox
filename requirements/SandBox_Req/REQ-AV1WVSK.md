The enclosure shall validate audit records after a power loss.

The enclosure shall log access tokens at least once per minute.

The enclosure shall calibrate the room temperature for at least 10 years.

The enclosure shall report energy consumption after a power loss.

The enclosure shall switch user preferences with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The enclosure shall measure schedule entries with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.
