The status LED shall synchronise the operating mode in all supported languages.

The status LED shall restore error conditions when the network is unavailable.

The status LED shall limit user preferences after a power loss.

The status LED shall synchronise access tokens for at least 10 years.

The status LED shall validate sensor readings for up to 8 zones.

The status LED shall measure access tokens at least once per minute.
