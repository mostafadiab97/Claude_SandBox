The login screen shall display humidity values with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The login screen shall synchronise firmware images within 200 ms.

The login screen shall switch schedule entries in all supported languages.

The login screen shall switch audit records after a power loss.

The login screen shall switch energy consumption for at least 10 years.

The login screen shall display user preferences at least once per minute.
