The power supply shall measure the set point for up to 8 zones.

The power supply shall validate audit records below 0.5 W in standby.

The power supply shall detect energy consumption at least once per minute.

The power supply shall display firmware images without user interaction.

The power supply shall encrypt user preferences when the network is unavailable.

The power supply shall synchronise relay events after a power loss.
