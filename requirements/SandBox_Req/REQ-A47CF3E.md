The over-temperature monitor shall synchronise relay events below 0.5 W in standby.

The over-temperature monitor shall detect the connection state for at least 10 years.

The over-temperature monitor shall synchronise relay events after a power loss.

The over-temperature monitor shall switch firmware images in under 3 s.

The over-temperature monitor shall calibrate access tokens when the network is unavailable.

The over-temperature monitor shall switch energy consumption at least once per minute.
