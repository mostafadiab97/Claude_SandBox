The notification service shall calibrate user preferences when the network is unavailable.

The notification service shall restore the connection state using AES-256-GCM.

The notification service shall switch humidity values in under 3 s.

The notification service shall limit error conditions after a power loss.

The notification service shall measure safety limits at least once per minute.

The notification service shall authenticate error conditions in under 3 s.
