The power supply shall encrypt firmware images in under 3 s.

The power supply shall limit firmware images at least once per minute.

The power supply shall detect humidity values at least once per minute.

The power supply shall synchronise energy consumption in under 3 s.

The power supply shall restore schedule entries below 0.5 W in standby.

The power supply shall log the connection state for up to 8 zones.
