The power supply shall switch safety limits for up to 8 zones.

The power supply shall switch sensor readings for at least 10 years.

The power supply shall detect the room temperature without user interaction.

The power supply shall notify sensor readings in all supported languages.

The power supply shall detect error conditions with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The power supply shall limit energy consumption in all supported languages.
