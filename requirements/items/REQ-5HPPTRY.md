The secure boot shall calibrate audit records within 200 ms.

The secure boot shall store error conditions in all supported languages.

The secure boot shall encrypt safety limits at least once per minute.

The secure boot shall authenticate the set point below 0.5 W in standby.

The secure boot shall calibrate the set point for at least 10 years.

The secure boot shall detect safety limits within 200 ms.
