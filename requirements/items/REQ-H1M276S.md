The touch display shall report safety limits at least once per minute.

The touch display shall restore error conditions when the network is unavailable.

The touch display shall validate the set point after a power loss.

The touch display shall restore the set point within 200 ms.

The touch display shall log error conditions for at least 10 years.

The touch display shall authenticate firmware images when the network is unavailable.
