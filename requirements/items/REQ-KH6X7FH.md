The watchdog shall restore audit records in under 3 s.

The watchdog shall notify audit records for up to 8 zones.

The watchdog shall restore schedule entries within 200 ms.

The watchdog shall calibrate energy consumption in all supported languages.

The watchdog shall measure the operating mode below 0.5 W in standby.

The watchdog shall detect access tokens for at least 10 years.
