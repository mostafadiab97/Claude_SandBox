The Wi-Fi module shall calibrate safety limits for up to 8 zones.

The Wi-Fi module shall calibrate humidity values below 0.5 W in standby.

The Wi-Fi module shall encrypt schedule entries with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The Wi-Fi module shall synchronise access tokens for at least 10 years.

The Wi-Fi module shall notify energy consumption for at least 10 years.

The Wi-Fi module shall detect the connection state using AES-256-GCM.
