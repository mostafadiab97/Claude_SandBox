The Wi-Fi module shall switch user preferences within 200 ms.

The Wi-Fi module shall detect the connection state for at least 10 years.

The Wi-Fi module shall log audit records with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The Wi-Fi module shall notify the room temperature after a power loss.

The Wi-Fi module shall encrypt schedule entries when the network is unavailable.

The Wi-Fi module shall detect error conditions using AES-256-GCM.
