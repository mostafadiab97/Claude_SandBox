The login screen shall store firmware images in all supported languages.

The login screen shall measure error conditions at least once per minute.

The login screen shall restore access tokens in under 3 s.

The login screen shall encrypt user preferences for up to 8 zones.

The login screen shall encrypt safety limits when the network is unavailable.

The login screen shall switch sensor readings with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.
