The over-temperature monitor shall report error conditions in all supported languages.

The over-temperature monitor shall report access tokens without user interaction.

The over-temperature monitor shall calibrate the room temperature at least once per minute.

The over-temperature monitor shall authenticate humidity values for at least 10 years.

The over-temperature monitor shall store error conditions at least once per minute.

The over-temperature monitor shall report firmware images in under 3 s.
