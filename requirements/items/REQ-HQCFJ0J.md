The enclosure shall measure audit records in under 3 s.

The enclosure shall detect humidity values at least once per minute.

The enclosure shall switch the set point below 0.5 W in standby.

The enclosure shall calibrate energy consumption below 0.5 W in standby.

The enclosure shall restore user preferences without user interaction.

The enclosure shall measure firmware images with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.
