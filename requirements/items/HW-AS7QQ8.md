The schedule editor shall measure error conditions below 0.5 W in standby.

The schedule editor shall encrypt the operating mode when the network is unavailable.

The schedule editor shall authenticate the connection state within 200 ms.

The schedule editor shall log user preferences for up to 8 zones.

The schedule editor shall log safety limits for at least 10 years.

The schedule editor shall log the room temperature after a power loss.
