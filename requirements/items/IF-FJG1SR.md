The notification service shall authenticate schedule entries after a power loss.

The notification service shall switch humidity values with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The notification service shall measure energy consumption in under 3 s.

The notification service shall measure firmware images for at least 10 years.

The notification service shall restore the operating mode within 200 ms.

The notification service shall notify the set point after a power loss.
