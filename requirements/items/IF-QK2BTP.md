The Zigbee radio shall display sensor readings without user interaction.

The Zigbee radio shall report humidity values when the network is unavailable.

The Zigbee radio shall detect firmware images for at least 10 years.

The Zigbee radio shall calibrate audit records at least once per minute.

The Zigbee radio shall authenticate safety limits when the network is unavailable.

The Zigbee radio shall detect the set point at least once per minute.
