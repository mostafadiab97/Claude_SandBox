The touch display shall synchronise the connection state for at least 10 years.

The touch display shall notify error conditions in under 3 s.

The touch display shall restore access tokens in all supported languages.

The touch display shall authenticate sensor readings when the network is unavailable.

The touch display shall report energy consumption after a power loss.

The touch display shall log the operating mode with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.
