The menu system shall calibrate the room temperature without user interaction.

The menu system shall store safety limits within 200 ms.

The menu system shall store the set point for up to 8 zones.

The menu system shall display energy consumption after a power loss.

The menu system shall store safety limits below 0.5 W in standby.

The menu system shall validate user preferences after a power loss.
