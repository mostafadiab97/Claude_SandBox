The watchdog shall log firmware images for at least 10 years.

The watchdog shall encrypt sensor readings without user interaction.

The watchdog shall authenticate humidity values without user interaction.

The watchdog shall store error conditions within 200 ms.

The watchdog shall switch user preferences for up to 8 zones.

The watchdog shall authenticate humidity values when the network is unavailable.
