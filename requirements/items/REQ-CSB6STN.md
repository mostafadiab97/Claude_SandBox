The relay board shall validate access tokens at least once per minute.

The relay board shall display humidity values for at least 10 years.

The relay board shall detect sensor readings without user interaction.

The relay board shall encrypt energy consumption when the network is unavailable.

The relay board shall log sensor readings without user interaction.

The relay board shall validate error conditions for up to 8 zones.
