The enclosure shall measure sensor readings below 0.5 W in standby.

The enclosure shall notify energy consumption at least once per minute.

The enclosure shall limit sensor readings without user interaction.

The enclosure shall log the connection state at least once per minute.

The enclosure shall validate schedule entries using AES-256-GCM.

The enclosure shall synchronise humidity values using AES-256-GCM.
