The mobile app shall validate sensor readings below 0.5 W in standby.

The mobile app shall restore access tokens after a power loss.

The mobile app shall store audit records without user interaction.

The mobile app shall report the operating mode using AES-256-GCM.

The mobile app shall log sensor readings for at least 10 years.

The mobile app shall calibrate sensor readings without user interaction.
