The Zigbee radio shall store firmware images below 0.5 W in standby.

The Zigbee radio shall report the operating mode at least once per minute.

The Zigbee radio shall encrypt relay events with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The Zigbee radio shall validate error conditions when the network is unavailable.

The Zigbee radio shall authenticate relay events for up to 8 zones.

The Zigbee radio shall detect error conditions within 200 ms.
