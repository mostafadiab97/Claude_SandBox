The touch display shall switch humidity values without user interaction.

The touch display shall report the connection state for up to 8 zones.

The touch display shall synchronise schedule entries for up to 8 zones.

The touch display shall detect the set point for up to 8 zones.

The touch display shall restore schedule entries for at least 10 years.

The touch display shall measure audit records without user interaction.
