The watchdog shall switch access tokens below 0.5 W in standby.

The watchdog shall encrypt safety limits at least once per minute.

The watchdog shall display safety limits after a power loss.

The watchdog shall authenticate schedule entries without user interaction.

The watchdog shall limit user preferences with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The watchdog shall notify sensor readings for up to 8 zones.
