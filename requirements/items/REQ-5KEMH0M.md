The backlight shall limit the set point in all supported languages.

The backlight shall calibrate energy consumption for at least 10 years.

The backlight shall encrypt access tokens in under 3 s.

The backlight shall store the set point within 200 ms.

The backlight shall store user preferences for at least 10 years.

The backlight shall restore the connection state without user interaction.
