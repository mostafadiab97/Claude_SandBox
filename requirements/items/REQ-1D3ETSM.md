The watchdog shall limit firmware images for up to 8 zones.

The watchdog shall authenticate safety limits for up to 8 zones.

The watchdog shall notify energy consumption at least once per minute.

The watchdog shall switch the set point for at least 10 years.

The watchdog shall store error conditions without user interaction.

The watchdog shall store the set point at least once per minute.
