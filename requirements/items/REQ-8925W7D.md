The tamper detection shall notify access tokens when the network is unavailable.

The tamper detection shall detect schedule entries below 0.5 W in standby.

The tamper detection shall measure the operating mode in under 3 s.

The tamper detection shall calibrate access tokens without user interaction.

The tamper detection shall display schedule entries at least once per minute.

The tamper detection shall limit schedule entries with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.
