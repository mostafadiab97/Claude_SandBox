The zone valve shall report the operating mode after a power loss.

The zone valve shall detect safety limits for up to 8 zones.

The zone valve shall calibrate relay events with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The zone valve shall restore error conditions without user interaction.

The zone valve shall encrypt error conditions after a power loss.

The zone valve shall restore relay events after a power loss.
