The Bluetooth LE link shall display energy consumption in under 3 s.

The Bluetooth LE link shall authenticate the room temperature below 0.5 W in standby.

The Bluetooth LE link shall restore access tokens in all supported languages.

The Bluetooth LE link shall display audit records in under 3 s.

The Bluetooth LE link shall authenticate firmware images with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The Bluetooth LE link shall restore firmware images at least once per minute.
