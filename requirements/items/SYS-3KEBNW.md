The home dashboard shall measure energy consumption at least once per minute.

The home dashboard shall report audit records in under 3 s.

The home dashboard shall log the room temperature for at least 10 years.

The home dashboard shall limit the set point with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The home dashboard shall store sensor readings when the network is unavailable.

The home dashboard shall restore schedule entries when the network is unavailable.
