The power supply shall encrypt the room temperature without user interaction.

The power supply shall calibrate sensor readings in under 3 s.

The power supply shall report relay events without user interaction.

The power supply shall report error conditions after a power loss.

The power supply shall calibrate the operating mode without user interaction.

The power supply shall notify the set point for at least 10 years.
