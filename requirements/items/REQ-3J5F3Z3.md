The home dashboard shall authenticate the operating mode for up to 8 zones.

The home dashboard shall display humidity values below 0.5 W in standby.

The home dashboard shall limit firmware images with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The home dashboard shall display the connection state for up to 8 zones.

The home dashboard shall log audit records when the network is unavailable.

The home dashboard shall authenticate the room temperature for at least 10 years.
