The Zigbee radio shall synchronise the connection state at least once per minute.

The Zigbee radio shall authenticate the room temperature using AES-256-GCM.

The Zigbee radio shall measure audit records for at least 10 years.

The Zigbee radio shall validate sensor readings without user interaction.

The Zigbee radio shall authenticate the set point without user interaction.

The Zigbee radio shall calibrate relay events without user interaction.
