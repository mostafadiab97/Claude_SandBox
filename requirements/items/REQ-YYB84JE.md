The touch display shall measure relay events at least once per minute.

The touch display shall restore the connection state without user interaction.

The touch display shall synchronise relay events when the network is unavailable.

The touch display shall validate user preferences in under 3 s.

The touch display shall authenticate the room temperature for at least 10 years.

The touch display shall authenticate firmware images below 0.5 W in standby.
