The battery backup shall display the connection state for at least 10 years.

The battery backup shall log relay events without user interaction.

The battery backup shall detect the operating mode in under 3 s.

The battery backup shall validate the room temperature at least once per minute.

The battery backup shall limit the room temperature for at least 10 years.

The battery backup shall limit user preferences after a power loss.
