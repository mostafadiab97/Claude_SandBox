The power supply shall report firmware images for up to 8 zones.

The power supply shall restore schedule entries in under 3 s.

The power supply shall display the set point using AES-256-GCM.

The power supply shall calibrate schedule entries after a power loss.

The power supply shall measure error conditions when the network is unavailable.

The power supply shall calibrate the operating mode using AES-256-GCM.
