The power supply shall limit access tokens for at least 10 years.

The power supply shall switch the operating mode using AES-256-GCM.

The power supply shall encrypt safety limits after a power loss.

The power supply shall encrypt schedule entries at least once per minute.

The power supply shall encrypt safety limits without user interaction.

The power supply shall restore error conditions for at least 10 years.
