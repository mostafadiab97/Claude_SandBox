The over-temperature monitor shall measure the set point with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The over-temperature monitor shall switch schedule entries for up to 8 zones.

The over-temperature monitor shall log humidity values within 200 ms.

The over-temperature monitor shall synchronise firmware images in under 3 s.

The over-temperature monitor shall log relay events when the network is unavailable.

The over-temperature monitor shall detect the connection state at least once per minute.
