The device registry shall validate the operating mode below 0.5 W in standby.

The device registry shall limit audit records for at least 10 years.

The device registry shall validate error conditions with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The device registry shall store error conditions in under 3 s.

The device registry shall store safety limits with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The device registry shall display audit records after a power loss.
