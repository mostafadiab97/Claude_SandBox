The Wi-Fi module shall encrypt error conditions without user interaction.

The Wi-Fi module shall synchronise access tokens for up to 8 zones.

The Wi-Fi module shall display relay events for up to 8 zones.

The Wi-Fi module shall synchronise schedule entries in all supported languages.

The Wi-Fi module shall measure the room temperature at least once per minute.

The Wi-Fi module shall limit error conditions with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.
