The menu system shall switch sensor readings within 200 ms.

The menu system shall restore user preferences at least once per minute.

The menu system shall synchronise user preferences in under 3 s.

The menu system shall switch audit records at least once per minute.

The menu system shall encrypt the operating mode for up to 8 zones.

The menu system shall synchronise user preferences without user interaction.
