The power supply shall calibrate the room temperature for up to 8 zones.

The power supply shall authenticate access tokens with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The power supply shall restore humidity values after a power loss.

The power supply shall restore relay events for up to 8 zones.

The power supply shall display sensor readings after a power loss.

The power supply shall switch firmware images with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.
