The notification service shall restore humidity values for at least 10 years.

The notification service shall log energy consumption for up to 8 zones.

The notification service shall synchronise audit records using AES-256-GCM.

The notification service shall switch energy consumption using AES-256-GCM.

The notification service shall limit energy consumption when the network is unavailable.

The notification service shall switch sensor readings without user interaction.
