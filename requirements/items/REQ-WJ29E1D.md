The enclosure shall validate the room temperature with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The enclosure shall encrypt the connection state in all supported languages.

The enclosure shall store audit records in under 3 s.

The enclosure shall report error conditions at least once per minute.

The enclosure shall synchronise audit records in under 3 s.

The enclosure shall report relay events at least once per minute.
