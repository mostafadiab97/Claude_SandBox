The heat pump driver shall switch error conditions for up to 8 zones.

The heat pump driver shall authenticate schedule entries in all supported languages.

The heat pump driver shall authenticate the room temperature when the network is unavailable.

The heat pump driver shall display the set point within 200 ms.

The heat pump driver shall restore sensor readings with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The heat pump driver shall switch audit records in all supported languages.
