The pairing process shall notify sensor readings in all supported languages.

The pairing process shall validate the room temperature at least once per minute.

The pairing process shall encrypt the connection state with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The pairing process shall synchronise relay events without user interaction.

The pairing process shall synchronise error conditions in all supported languages.

The pairing process shall limit the room temperature at least once per minute.
