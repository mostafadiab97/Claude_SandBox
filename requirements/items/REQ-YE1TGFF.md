The zone valve shall limit firmware images without user interaction.

The zone valve shall switch firmware images for up to 8 zones.

The zone valve shall report error conditions in all supported languages.

The zone valve shall restore the operating mode with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The zone valve shall log firmware images below 0.5 W in standby.

The zone valve shall measure the operating mode after a power loss.
