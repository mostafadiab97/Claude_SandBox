The over-temperature monitor shall display the operating mode at least once per minute.

The over-temperature monitor shall display user preferences in all supported languages.

The over-temperature monitor shall detect error conditions for at least 10 years.

The over-temperature monitor shall validate user preferences without user interaction.

The over-temperature monitor shall restore the set point for up to 8 zones.

The over-temperature monitor shall encrypt error conditions after a power loss.
