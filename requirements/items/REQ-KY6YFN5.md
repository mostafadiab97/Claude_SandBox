The backlight shall report sensor readings without user interaction.

The backlight shall calibrate energy consumption at least once per minute.

The backlight shall switch energy consumption for at least 10 years.

The backlight shall switch the operating mode when the network is unavailable.

The backlight shall notify sensor readings with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The backlight shall calibrate the room temperature below 0.5 W in standby.
