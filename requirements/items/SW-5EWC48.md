The heat pump driver shall synchronise the operating mode when the network is unavailable.

The heat pump driver shall restore access tokens in under 3 s.

The heat pump driver shall log schedule entries below 0.5 W in standby.

The heat pump driver shall display energy consumption in all supported languages.

The heat pump driver shall display error conditions for up to 8 zones.

The heat pump driver shall authenticate humidity values when the network is unavailable.
