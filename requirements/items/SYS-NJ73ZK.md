The pairing process shall synchronise sensor readings in under 3 s.

The pairing process shall validate audit records with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The pairing process shall store access tokens without user interaction.

The pairing process shall validate schedule entries for at least 10 years.

The pairing process shall validate the room temperature with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The pairing process shall restore firmware images within 200 ms.
