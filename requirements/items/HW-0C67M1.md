The battery backup shall switch sensor readings with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The battery backup shall notify humidity values with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The battery backup shall notify the room temperature using AES-256-GCM.

The battery backup shall store energy consumption for up to 8 zones.

The battery backup shall authenticate energy consumption with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The battery backup shall limit the set point for at least 10 years.
