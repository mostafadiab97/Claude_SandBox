The power supply shall validate user preferences without user interaction.

The power supply shall store the set point when the network is unavailable.

The power supply shall measure the room temperature for up to 8 zones.

The power supply shall display safety limits for up to 8 zones.

The power supply shall report sensor readings with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The power supply shall notify the operating mode at least once per minute.
