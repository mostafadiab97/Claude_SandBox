The status LED shall store the set point after a power loss.

The status LED shall synchronise humidity values for up to 8 zones.

The status LED shall display the room temperature with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The status LED shall measure sensor readings for at least 10 years.

The status LED shall report humidity values in all supported languages.

The status LED shall calibrate the set point below 0.5 W in standby.
