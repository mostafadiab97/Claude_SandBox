The cloud backend shall notify the set point after a power loss.

The cloud backend shall notify sensor readings in under 3 s.

The cloud backend shall notify error conditions below 0.5 W in standby.

The cloud backend shall switch energy consumption for up to 8 zones.

The cloud backend shall authenticate the operating mode for at least 10 years.

The cloud backend shall log the connection state after a power loss.
