The heat pump driver shall restore user preferences for at least 10 years.

The heat pump driver shall log sensor readings within 200 ms.

The heat pump driver shall encrypt the operating mode after a power loss.

The heat pump driver shall notify safety limits when the network is unavailable.

The heat pump driver shall calibrate user preferences for up to 8 zones.

The heat pump driver shall store schedule entries using AES-256-GCM.
