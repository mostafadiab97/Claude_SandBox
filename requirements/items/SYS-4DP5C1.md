The zone valve shall authenticate access tokens for up to 8 zones.

The zone valve shall store error conditions within 200 ms.

The zone valve shall switch the set point within 200 ms.

The zone valve shall log firmware images with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The zone valve shall restore firmware images in under 3 s.

The zone valve shall switch energy consumption within 200 ms.
