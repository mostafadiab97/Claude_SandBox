The Bluetooth LE link shall synchronise firmware images below 0.5 W in standby.

The Bluetooth LE link shall calibrate relay events for up to 8 zones.

The Bluetooth LE link shall measure the connection state with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The Bluetooth LE link shall restore relay events in all supported languages.

The Bluetooth LE link shall notify firmware images when the network is unavailable.
