The Zigbee radio shall report humidity values without user interaction.

The Zigbee radio shall synchronise relay events after a power loss.

The Zigbee radio shall calibrate firmware images after a power loss.

The Zigbee radio shall log safety limits for at least 10 years.

The Zigbee radio shall calibrate access tokens in under 3 s.

The Zigbee radio shall display energy consumption when the network is unavailable.
