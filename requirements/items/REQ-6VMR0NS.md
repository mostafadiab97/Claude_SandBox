The enclosure shall encrypt access tokens without user interaction.

The enclosure shall notify energy consumption using AES-256-GCM.

The enclosure shall limit the operating mode with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The enclosure shall detect error conditions within 200 ms.

The enclosure shall limit the operating mode for up to 8 zones.

The enclosure shall detect sensor readings after a power loss.
