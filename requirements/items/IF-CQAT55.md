The boiler interface shall restore the operating mode below 0.5 W in standby.

The boiler interface shall switch sensor readings for at least 10 years.

The boiler interface shall display sensor readings for at least 10 years.

The boiler interface shall detect error conditions without user interaction.

The boiler interface shall notify error conditions for at least 10 years.

The boiler interface shall validate relay events within 200 ms.
