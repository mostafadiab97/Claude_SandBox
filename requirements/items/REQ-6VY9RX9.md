The touch display shall synchronise humidity values using AES-256-GCM.

The touch display shall switch safety limits after a power loss.

The touch display shall restore user preferences below 0.5 W in standby.

The touch display shall authenticate the set point for at least 10 years.

The touch display shall switch the operating mode at least once per minute.

The touch display shall calibrate audit records after a power loss.
