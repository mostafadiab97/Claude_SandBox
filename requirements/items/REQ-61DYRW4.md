The device registry shall report schedule entries when the network is unavailable.

The device registry shall store humidity values in under 3 s.

The device registry shall measure sensor readings within 200 ms.

The device registry shall report firmware images for at least 10 years.

The device registry shall display the connection state in all supported languages.

The device registry shall limit the room temperature in all supported languages.
