The heating controller shall switch the connection state in all supported languages.

The heating controller shall report the room temperature within 200 ms.

The heating controller shall log error conditions for up to 8 zones.

The heating controller shall restore schedule entries at least once per minute.

The heating controller shall calibrate access tokens below 0.5 W in standby.

The heating controller shall restore safety limits in under 3 s.
