The heating controller shall detect user preferences when the network is unavailable.

The heating controller shall limit audit records for at least 10 years.

The heating controller shall store access tokens using AES-256-GCM.

The heating controller shall limit energy consumption below 0.5 W in standby.

The heating controller shall display the room temperature with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The heating controller shall restore audit records within 200 ms.
