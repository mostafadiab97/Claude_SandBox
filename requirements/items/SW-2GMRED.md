The boiler interface shall notify safety limits with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The boiler interface shall switch humidity values for up to 8 zones.

The boiler interface shall notify audit records in under 3 s.

The boiler interface shall log error conditions at least once per minute.

The boiler interface shall store sensor readings using AES-256-GCM.

The boiler interface shall switch user preferences without user interaction.
