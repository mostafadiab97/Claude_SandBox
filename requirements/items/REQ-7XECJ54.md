The tamper detection shall authenticate schedule entries when the network is unavailable.

The tamper detection shall report safety limits within 200 ms.

The tamper detection shall store safety limits within 200 ms.

The tamper detection shall notify humidity values after a power loss.

The tamper detection shall display firmware images when the network is unavailable.

The tamper detection shall limit the connection state with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.
