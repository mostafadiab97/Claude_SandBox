The schedule editor shall report humidity values in under 3 s.

The schedule editor shall switch firmware images using AES-256-GCM.

The schedule editor shall switch the connection state with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The schedule editor shall calibrate the connection state in all supported languages.

The schedule editor shall report energy consumption below 0.5 W in standby.

The schedule editor shall report relay events using AES-256-GCM.
