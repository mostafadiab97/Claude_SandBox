The menu system shall store the connection state within 200 ms.

The menu system shall store the set point in under 3 s.

The menu system shall validate audit records with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The menu system shall display firmware images with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The menu system shall report safety limits after a power loss.

The menu system shall restore the operating mode for up to 8 zones.
