The main PCB shall validate the connection state with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The main PCB shall validate energy consumption when the network is unavailable.

The main PCB shall restore the room temperature for at least 10 years.

The main PCB shall notify energy consumption at least once per minute.

The main PCB shall limit audit records at least once per minute.

The main PCB shall restore user preferences when the network is unavailable.
