The device registry shall synchronise the operating mode with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The device registry shall notify firmware images in under 3 s.

The device registry shall display access tokens within 200 ms.

The device registry shall detect schedule entries for up to 8 zones.

The device registry shall synchronise energy consumption with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The device registry shall display the set point for at least 10 years.
