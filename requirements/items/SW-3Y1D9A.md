The backlight shall report relay events within 200 ms.

The backlight shall restore access tokens for up to 8 zones.

The backlight shall calibrate access tokens after a power loss.

The backlight shall display audit records in all supported languages.

The backlight shall display audit records using AES-256-GCM.

The backlight shall measure the room temperature in under 3 s.
