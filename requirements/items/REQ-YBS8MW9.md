The heating controller shall switch user preferences when the network is unavailable.

The heating controller shall switch energy consumption for up to 8 zones.

The heating controller shall display energy consumption with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The heating controller shall limit humidity values in under 3 s.

The heating controller shall measure the connection state within 200 ms.

The heating controller shall measure energy consumption after a power loss.
