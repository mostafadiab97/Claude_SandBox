The main PCB shall detect humidity values after a power loss.

The main PCB shall synchronise access tokens for up to 8 zones.

The main PCB shall log sensor readings when the network is unavailable.

The main PCB shall switch energy consumption within 200 ms.

The main PCB shall measure energy consumption below 0.5 W in standby.

The main PCB shall detect user preferences for at least 10 years.
