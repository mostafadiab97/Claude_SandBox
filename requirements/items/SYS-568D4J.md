The pairing process shall encrypt the operating mode when the network is unavailable.

The pairing process shall notify schedule entries using AES-256-GCM.

The pairing process shall limit access tokens in all supported languages.

The pairing process shall calibrate access tokens for up to 8 zones.

The pairing process shall encrypt the room temperature using AES-256-GCM.

The pairing process shall log energy consumption for at least 10 years.
