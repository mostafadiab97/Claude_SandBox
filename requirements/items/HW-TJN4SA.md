The battery backup shall measure humidity values after a power loss.

The battery backup shall validate safety limits with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.

The battery backup shall store energy consumption in under 3 s.

The battery backup shall store user preferences for at least 10 years.

The battery backup shall notify sensor readings for at least 10 years.

The battery backup shall limit the set point for at least 10 years.
