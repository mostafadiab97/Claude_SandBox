The pairing process shall measure relay events in all supported languages.

The pairing process shall encrypt access tokens when the network is unavailable.

The pairing process shall synchronise access tokens for at least 10 years.

The pairing process shall encrypt relay events within 200 ms.

The pairing process shall calibrate access tokens when the network is unavailable.

The pairing process shall store firmware images with an accuracy of ±0.5 °C.
