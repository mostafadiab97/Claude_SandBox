The Bluetooth LE link shall encrypt humidity values after a power loss.

The Bluetooth LE link shall store user preferences in under 3 s.

The Bluetooth LE link shall calibrate access tokens in under 3 s.

The Bluetooth LE link shall switch error conditions when the network is unavailable.

The Bluetooth LE link shall measure the operating mode at least once per minute.

The Bluetooth LE link shall encrypt the operating mode below 0.5 W in standby.
