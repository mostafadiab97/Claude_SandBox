The boiler interface shall store firmware images for at least 10 years.

The boiler interface shall calibrate the operating mode in under 3 s.

The boiler interface shall store the room temperature within 200 ms.

The boiler interface shall switch error conditions for at least 10 years.

The boiler interface shall report humidity values below 0.5 W in standby.

The boiler interface shall synchronise the operating mode within 200 ms.
