The over-temperature monitor shall restore energy consumption within 200 ms.

The over-temperature monitor shall limit relay events without user interaction.

The over-temperature monitor shall notify schedule entries in under 3 s.

The over-temperature monitor shall restore relay events for up to 8 zones.

The over-temperature monitor shall detect error conditions for at least 10 years.

The over-temperature monitor shall restore error conditions after a power loss.
